## Scope and extraction notes

- The source README has a separate **Must Know** section. It is intentionally excluded because this workspace is limited to the 14 requested topics.
- Every top-level list entry within each requested source section is included, including entries phrased as labels rather than sentences ending in a question mark. This avoids omitting source prompts; wording and order are preserved.
- Nested `Answer:` resource links and introductory material are not interview prompts and are not copied.
- No unresolved section-boundary or extraction ambiguities were found in the inspected source README.
- Topic 15 is a separate, research-informed supplement synthesized from current engineering documentation. It is not extracted from or attributed to the original source repository, and it does not claim to rank interview frequency.
- Evaluation already has extensive coverage in topics 09 and 13, including evaluation datasets, judge-based evaluation, RAG/agent evaluation, online and continuous evaluation, statistical comparisons, and implementation. Topic 15 avoids repeating those questions.
