## Research and confidence conventions

- **Official process information** comes from the company's public careers material. It can change and does not guarantee a particular sequence for every role or location.
- **Publicly reported patterns** are attributed to accessible candidate-reconstruction sources. Such reports are anecdotal, may be role- or level-specific, and are not official hiring rubrics.
- **Practice questions** are original prompts derived from the role scope, public product documentation, and common engineering responsibilities. They are not represented as verbatim or guaranteed interview questions.
- Check the exact job description and ask the recruiter which rounds, level expectations, and product area apply to your application.
